# The Meal-Prep System: Prep, Cook, Store, Reheat

How to turn a shopping bag into 14+ ready meals in about 2–3 hours.

## 1. Weekly cycle

| Day | Task | Time |
|---|---|---|
| Thu/Fri | Plan meals, check the ad, write the list | 15 min |
| Sat/Sun morning | Shop | 45–60 min |
| Sun | **Batch cook day** | 2–3 h (see below) |
| Sun–Wed | Eat from the fridge | – |
| Wed–Thu | Portions for Thu–Sat come from the freezer | – |
| Daily | Assemble and reheat | 5–7 min |

Cooked food lasts **3–4 days** in the fridge (USDA). For a 7-day week, freeze days 4–7 the night you cook and thaw in the fridge the night before.

## 2. Equipment

**Essential:** 2–4 half-sheet pans, foil or parchment, large pot with lid (rice), large skillet, instant-read thermometer (about $15; the best safety and quality tool), sharp chef's knife, cutting boards (raw meat separate), digital scale, measuring cups and spoons, containers (4–5 cup, microwave-safe, 10–14), mason jars.
**Nice to have:** slow cooker or pressure cooker, rice cooker, air fryer, kitchen shears (spatchcock and open packages), freezer bags, label tape and marker, blender.

## 3. Batch cook day workflow (about 2 hours)

1. **Read all recipes and set out ingredients** (mise en place).
2. **Start the longest, hands-off items first:** heat oven, slow cooker or pressure cooker.
3. **Prep meat while the oven heats:** pat dry, season, spread on pans (don't crowd).
4. **Oven in first, then rice on the stove, then jars/portions:** overlap tasks.
5. **Use downtime:** cut vegetables, boil eggs, make sauce, wash dishes.
6. **Rest and cool:** cooked food out of the oven cools on the pan; rice spread thin on a sheet pan.
7. **Portion and store within 2 hours** of cooking: shallow containers, lids on when cool.
8. **Label:** food, date cooked, "eat by" date.
9. **Clean as you go.**

### Sample timeline (12 lb thighs + rice + oats + eggs)
| Time | Action |
|---|---|
| 0:00 | Oven to 425°F; pat and season chicken; set up 4 pans |
| 0:15 | Chicken in (two racks; swap at 20 min); boil eggs |
| 0:20 | Rice on to boil, then low 18 min; overnight oat jars assembled |
| 0:45 | Rice off heat, rest 5 min; spread on sheet pan |
| 0:55 | Chicken to 175°F; rest 10 min |
| 1:10 | Peel skin, portion chicken 5.5 oz per container, add rice, veg |
| 1:30 | Cool uncovered 15–20 min, lid and refrigerate/freeze |
| 1:45 | Clean up |

Total hands-on time is about 45–60 min; the rest is waiting.

## 4. Assemble, don't cook, on weekdays

Bowl formula: **protein + starch + vegetable + sauce**.
- 5.5 oz protein, 1 cup rice, 1/2 cup vegetables, 1 tbsp sauce = about 565 kcal, 45 g protein (chicken bowl, recipe `chicken-rice-bowl` in `data/cookbook/meal-prep.json`).
- Change the sauce, not the process: teriyaki, buffalo, chimichurri, peanut, curry, salsa.

## 5. Containers and layout
- Keep sauces separate from rice so it doesn't get soggy (or mix in and reheat).
- Roast vegetables reheat well; leafy salads don't.
- Glass or BPA-free containers, 3–4 cups.
- Rice: cool in a thin layer, 2 hours max, then refrigerate.

## 6. Reheating
- **Microwave:** cover, add 1 tbsp water, 90 s, stir, 30 s more; reach 165°F.
- **Oven:** 325°F covered with foil 15–20 min for chicken pieces.
- **Skillet:** for crumbles and stir-fries, 3–5 min.
- **Frozen portions:** thaw in the fridge overnight, or microwave on defrost then heat.

## 7. Cooking fundamentals

- **Preheat fully** (ovens take 15–20 min).
- **Dry the surface** for browning.
- **Salt early.**
- **Don't crowd the pan.**
- **Rest meat** before cutting.
- **Taste and adjust** acid and salt at the end.
- **Use a thermometer:** doneness by time is a guess.
- **Knife safety:** stable cutting board (damp towel underneath), claw grip, sharp knife.
- **Mise en place** (everything prepped before heat).

## 8. Time-savers
- Sheet-pan everything: chicken, potatoes and vegetables simultaneously.
- Pressure cooker or slow cooker for shredded meat, beans, soup.
- Rice cooker for grains while you do other tasks.
- Pre-chop onions and garlic once for the week.
- Double a recipe and freeze half.

## 9. Troubleshooting

| Problem | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Dry chicken breast | overcooked, no brine | pull at 160°F, brine, poach, or switch to thigh |
| Soggy skin | steamed or refrigerated | dry brine, rack, high heat; peel skin for storage |
| Mushy rice reheated | too much water or not cooled quickly | 1.4–1.5:1 water, cool flat |
| Bland food | under-salted, no acid | salt earlier, add lemon or vinegar |
| Everything tastes the same by Thursday | one flavor | change sauces daily; freeze half |
| Bored | – | see [`seasoning-and-sauces.md`](seasoning-and-sauces.md) |
| Freezer burn | air | remove air, double-wrap |
| Too little food | poor yield math | see [`portions-and-scaling.md`](portions-and-scaling.md) |
