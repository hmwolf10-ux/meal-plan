# Grains, Starches and Vegetables

## Rice

| Type | Water:rice (stovetop) | Time | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **White long-grain** | 1.5:1 (rinse first) | boil, then low 18 min, rest 5 | cheapest carb; cooked 130 kcal, 2.7 g P, 28.2 g C per 100 g (USDA reference) |
| **Jasmine / basmati** | 1.25–1.5:1 | 15–18 min | fragrant; basmati is drier |
| **Brown** | 2:1 | 40–45 min | more fiber, chewier |
| **Sushi / short-grain** | 1.1–1.25:1 | 15 min | sticky |
| **Parboiled (converted)** | 2:1 | 20–25 min | forgiving for large batches |
| **Wild rice blend** | 3:1 | 45–55 min | |

**1 cup dry ≈ 3 cups cooked.** Rinse until water is mostly clear (removes surface starch). Cool within 2 hours (rice can carry *Bacillus cereus*). Reheat until steaming.
**Rice cooker:** same ratio; hands-off. **Oven rice:** 1.5:1 water in covered dish at 375°F for 25–30 min.
**Endurance note:** white rice is a low-fiber, easily digested carb preferred close to long efforts; brown rice is better in general daily meals.

## Oats
- **Rolled oats:** 1/2–3/4 cup dry per serving; ~389 kcal, 16.9 g P, 66.3 g C per 100 g dry (USDA reference).
- **Overnight oats:** oats + liquid 1:1 to 1:1.25 by volume + yogurt; refrigerate 4+ h; keeps 3–4 days; do not freeze.
- **Baked oatmeal and oat cups:** batch-friendly, freeze 2–3 months.

## Pasta
- 2 oz (56 g) dry = 1 serving, about 1 cup cooked; 1 lb dry ≈ 8 servings.
- Boil in salted water (about 1 tbsp kosher salt per 4 quarts), 1–2 min under package time for reheating later; toss with a little oil or sauce.
- Keeps 3–4 days; sauces absorb.

## Potatoes and starchy veg
| Food | Method | Time |
|---|---|---|
| Russet | roast 425°F, 3/4 in cubes | 30–35 min |
| Baked whole | 425°F | 45–60 min |
| Sweet potato cubes | 425°F | 25–30 min |
| Boiled potatoes | cold water start, simmer | 15–20 min |
| Air-fried | 400°F | 15–20 min |

Raw russet: 79 kcal, 2.1 g P, 18.1 g C per 100 g (USDA reference). Cheap, filling, batch-friendly.

## Bread and tortillas
Store bread in the freezer; toast from frozen. Tortillas keep a week in the fridge; freeze for longer.

## Vegetables: how to cook

|---|---|---|---|---|
| Broccoli | 18–22 min | 4–5 min | 5–6 min | cut florets uniform |
| Cauliflower | 25–30 min | 5–6 min | – | roast for browning |
| Carrots | 30–35 min | 6–8 min | – | halve lengthwise |
| Green beans | 12–15 min | 4–5 min | 5–6 min | |
| Brussels sprouts | 25–30 min (halved) | 6 min | – | |
| Zucchini | 15–20 min | 3 min | 4–5 min | high water content |
| Bell pepper | 20 min | – | 5 min | |
| Onion | 30–40 min | – | 8–10 min | caramelize 30–45 min |
| Asparagus | 10–12 min | 3 min | 4–5 min | |
| Spinach | – | wilts 1 min | 1–2 min | |
| Kale | 10–15 min (chips) | 5–8 min | 5–8 min | |
| Mushrooms | 20 min | – | 8 min | dry sauté first |
| Cabbage | 20–25 min (wedges) | 8–10 min | 8–10 min | very cheap |
| Butternut squash | 25–35 min | – | – | |
| Corn | – | 5–7 min | – | |

Frozen vegetables: cook straight from frozen; nutritionally comparable, often cheaper and less wasteful. Microwave 3–4 min or roast frozen at 450°F for 20–25 min.

## Storage

|---|---|---|
| Cooked vegetables | 3–5 days | 8–12 months (blanched raw) |
| Raw hardy veg (carrots, cabbage) | 2–4 weeks | blanch first |
| Leafy greens | 3–7 days | – |
| Cooked grains | 3–4 days | 1–3 months |

## Fiber note
Fiber is healthy but slows digestion; near intense endurance sessions choose low-fiber carbs (white rice, bananas, potatoes without skin, white bread).
