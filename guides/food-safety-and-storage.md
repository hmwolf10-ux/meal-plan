# Food Safety, Thawing and Storage

Rules from the USDA Food Safety and Inspection Service (FSIS). Refrigerator at or below **40°F**, freezer at **0°F**.

## The two-hour rule
Refrigerate cooked food within **2 hours** (1 hour if above 90°F). Between **40°F and 140°F** bacteria multiply fastest.

## Safe minimum internal temperatures

| Food | °F | Rest |
|---|---|---|
| All poultry (chicken, turkey, duck, ground poultry) | 165 | – |
| Ground beef, pork, lamb, veal | 160 | – |
| Beef, pork, veal, lamb: steaks, chops, roasts | 145 | 3 min |
| Fish and shellfish | 145 (flesh opaque and flakes) | – |
| Eggs cooked for dishes; egg dishes | 160 | – |
| Leftovers and casseroles | 165 | – |
| Fully cooked ham, reheated | 140 | – |

**Quality temps** (not safety): thigh and drumstick taste best at 175–185°F; beef steak at 130–135°F medium-rare (USDA lists 145°F as the safe minimum, so know the tradeoff).

## Refrigerator and freezer times

| Food | Refrigerator (raw) | Freezer (raw), best quality | Cooked leftovers |
|---|---|---|---|
| Chicken or turkey, pieces | 1–2 days | 9 months | 3–4 days fridge, 4 months freezer |
| Chicken or turkey, whole | 1–2 days | 1 year | same |
| Ground meat and poultry | 1–2 days | 3–4 months | same |
| Beef steaks, roasts | 3–5 days | 4–12 months | same |
| Pork chops, roasts | 3–5 days | 4–12 months | same |
| Bacon | 1 week | 1 month | – |
| Fresh fish (lean) | 1–2 days | 6 months | 3–4 days |
| Fresh fish (fatty) | 1–2 days | 2–3 months | 3–4 days |
| Shrimp | 1–2 days | 3–6 months | 3–4 days |
| Eggs in shell | 3–5 weeks | do not freeze in shell | – |
| Hard-boiled eggs | – | do not freeze | 1 week |
| Cooked rice and pasta | – | 1–3 months | 3–4 days (cool within 1–2 hours; rice can carry *Bacillus cereus*) |
| Soups and stews | – | 2–3 months | 3–4 days |
| Cooked beans/lentils | – | 6 months | 3–5 days |
| Overnight oats | – | do not freeze | 3–4 days |

Food frozen at 0°F stays safe indefinitely; the times above are for quality.

## Thawing safely
1. **Refrigerator:** best; allow ~24 h per 4–5 lb; cook within 1–2 days after thawing (raw poultry/ground).
2. **Cold water:** sealed bag, cold tap water, change every 30 min; ~30 min per lb; cook immediately.
3. **Microwave:** defrost setting; cook immediately.
4. **Never** thaw on the counter. Food cooked from frozen takes about 50% longer.

## Cross-contamination
- Separate cutting boards for raw meat and ready-to-eat food.
- Wash hands 20 seconds after handling raw meat.
- Do not wash raw poultry.
- Keep raw meat on the bottom fridge shelf.
- Do not reuse plates or marinades that touched raw meat unless boiled.

## Batch-prep storage practices
- Cool in shallow containers, rice spread thin on a sheet pan.
- **Label every container with the date cooked**; use within 3–4 days or freeze.
- Portion before freezing; flat bags thaw faster.
- Freeze meals you will not eat by day 3 on day 1.
- Reheat to 165°F, stir, and check the center.
- A plan cooked Sunday should be eaten Sunday–Wednesday from the fridge; freeze Thursday–Saturday portions immediately.
