# Fish and Seafood Guide

USDA safe minimum for fish and shellfish is **145°F** or flesh opaque and separating easily with a fork. Cook fish gently and quickly; most fish is done in 10 minutes per inch of thickness at 400–425°F.

## Common fish and macros (raw, USDA SR Legacy reference values)

| Fish | kcal / P / F per 100 g | Character | Best method |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Salmon (Atlantic, farmed)** | ~208 / 20.4 / 13.4 | rich, fatty, omega-3 | roast, pan-sear skin-on, grill, poach |
| **Cod** | ~82 / 17.8 / 0.7 | lean, mild, flaky | bake, pan-sear, fish tacos, chowder |
| **Tilapia** | ~96 / 20.1 / 1.7 | lean, mild, cheap | pan-fry, bake |
| **Shrimp** | ~85 / 20.1 / 0.5 | lean, quick | sauté 2–3 min per side, grill, roast |
| **Canned light tuna (in water)** | ~116 / 25.5 / 0.8 (drained) | shelf-stable | bowls, salads, patties |
| **Canned salmon** | ~140 / 20 / 6 | bones edible (calcium) | patties, salads |
| **Sardines (canned)** | ~208 / 24.6 / 11.5 | oily, strong | toast, pasta |
| **Trout** | ~119 / 20.5 / 3.5 | mild | pan-fry, bake |
| **Mahi-mahi, halibut** | lean | firm | grill, pan-sear |
| **Scallops** | lean | sweet | hot sear 2 min per side |

Numbers are reference values; re-check on FoodData Central for tracking.

## Buying
- **Fresh:** clear eyes, firm flesh, no strong odor.
- **Frozen fish** is often fresher than "fresh", as it is frozen at sea, and cheaper; thaw overnight in the fridge.
- **Farmed vs wild:** farmed salmon is fattier and cheaper; wild is leaner.
- **Mercury:** eat light tuna over albacore; limit albacore to about 1 serving per week; pregnant people should follow FDA guidance.
- **Budget:** canned tuna and salmon, frozen tilapia/cod/pollock, frozen shrimp on sale.

## Methods

| Method | Setting | Time |
|---|---|---|
| **Roast / bake** | 400–425°F | fillet 1 inch thick: 10–14 min |
| **Pan-sear** | medium-high, dry surface | 3–4 min skin-side down, 2–3 min flip |
| **Grill** | medium-high, oiled grate | 4–6 min per side (salmon steaks); use a foil sheet or basket for delicate fish |
| **Poach** | simmer in broth, wine or milk | 8–12 min |
| **Steam** | over simmering water, en papillote 400°F | 8–15 min |
| **Broil** | 6 in from heat | 6–8 min, no flip |
| **Air fry** | 400°F | 8–12 min |
| **Fry / beer-batter** | 350–375°F | 3–5 min |
| **Shrimp** | sauté / roast 425°F 6–8 min | opaque and curled to a C, not an O |
| **Canned** | no cook | drain well |

## Seasoning
Lemon, dill, capers, garlic, butter, soy-ginger, miso glaze, Cajun rub, Old Bay, harissa, chili-lime, everything-bagel, pesto. Salt 15 min ahead; marinate no more than 30 min in acid.

## Storage
Raw: 1–2 days fridge; cooked 3–4 days. Freeze raw lean fish up to 6 months, fatty fish 2–3 months.
