# Seasoning, Rubs, Marinades and Sauces

How to make the same batch protein taste different all week. Spice amounts are for about **5 lb (2.3 kg)** of bone-in meat or **2 lb (900 g)** ground meat unless noted; halve for boneless, since it has no bone weight.

## Salt: the one that matters

| Salt | Per lb of raw meat |
|---|---|
| Diamond Crystal kosher | about 3/4 tsp |
| Morton kosher | about 1/2 tsp |
| Table salt | about 1/3–1/2 tsp |
| Enhanced (pre-brined) chicken | skip or halve |

Dry brine ahead: salt 30 min to 24 h before cooking, uncovered in the fridge.

## The spice shelf

**Buy first (covers ~80% of rubs):** kosher salt, black pepper, garlic powder, onion powder, smoked paprika, chili powder, cumin, dried oregano.
**Second wave:** cayenne, thyme, coriander, turmeric, curry powder, Italian seasoning, red pepper flakes, cinnamon.
**Specialty:** allspice, garam masala, five-spice, za'atar, sumac, gochugaru, ancho powder, chipotle powder, smoked salt.
**Fresh finishers:** lemon and lime, cilantro, parsley, scallion, ginger, garlic.
**Cheap shopping tip:** buy spices in the international or bulk aisle; a jar of cumin costs a fraction there.

## Flavor architecture (how to build your own)

1. **Salt** (soy, fish sauce, salt).
2. **Fat** (oil, butter, coconut milk, sesame oil).
3. **Acid** (lemon, lime, vinegar, yogurt).
4. **Sweet** (honey, brown sugar, maple, fruit).
5. **Heat** (chili, cayenne, gochujang, hot sauce).
6. **Aromatics** (garlic, onion, ginger, herbs).
7. **Umami** (soy, fish sauce, tomato paste, mushrooms, parmesan).

Pick one from at least four groups. A sugary component burns above ~400°F: add it late or lower the oven.

## Storage of blends and marinades

- Dry rubs keep 6 months in an airtight jar; label with date.
- Marinades: refrigerate; use within 3–4 days; **do not reuse** raw-meat marinade unless boiled.
- Sauces with dairy: 3–4 days refrigerated.
