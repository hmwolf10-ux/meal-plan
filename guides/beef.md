# The Complete Beef Guide

Macros for ground beef are USDA SR Legacy standard values (reference, not re-verified this session). Doneness temps: USDA safe minimum for steaks and roasts is 145°F with a 3-minute rest; ground beef 160°F.

## Ground beef ratios

| Ratio (lean/fat) | Raw kcal / P / F per 100 g | Best for | Avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| 73/27 | ~332 / 14.5 / 28 | griddled burgers | bowls (greasy) |
| **80/20** (chuck) | ~254 / 17.2 / 20 | burgers, meatballs, taco meat (drain half the fat) | – |
| 85/15 | ~215 / 18.6 / 15 | meatloaf, stuffed peppers, meatballs | – |
| **90/10** (sirloin) | ~176 / 20 / 10 | chili, tacos in sauce, pasta sauce | burgers: dry |
| 93/7 | ~152 / 21.4 / 7 | sauces and chili only | burgers: dense, dry |

Fat renders out; draining a pan of 80/20 removes roughly a third of the fat. Ratio changes shrinkage: 80/20 loses more weight than 93/7.

**Cooking ground beef:** medium-high skillet, leave 1–2 min to brown, break up, cook to 160°F (~8–10 min). Lean grinds need a splash of water or broth.

## Whole cuts by area

| Primal | Cuts | Character | Best cooking |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Chuck** (shoulder) | chuck roast, chuck eye, shoulder steak, flat iron | tough, flavorful, collagen-rich | braise, slow cooker, pressure cook; flat iron grills |
| **Brisket** | flat, point | very tough, fatty | smoke 10–14 h or braise 3–4 h |
| **Rib** | ribeye, prime rib, short ribs | fatty, tender | sear/grill, roast; short ribs braise |
| **Loin** | strip (NY), T-bone, porterhouse, tenderloin | tender | grill, sear, roast |
| **Sirloin** | top sirloin, tri-tip | lean, mid-tender | grill, roast |
| **Round** (rear) | top round, bottom round, eye of round, rump | very lean, tough | braise, roast and slice thin, jerky |
| **Flank / plate** | flank, skirt, hanger | chewy, flavorful | high heat, marinate, slice against the grain |
| **Shank** | shank, oxtail | tough, gelatinous | braise, soup |

### Budget tiers
1. **Cheapest:** chuck roast, ground beef, round roast, shank, stew meat.
2. **Mid:** flank, sirloin, tri-tip, skirt.
3. **Splurge:** ribeye, strip, tenderloin.

## Doneness (steak)

| Doneness | Pull at | Final after rest |
|---|---|---|
| Rare | 120–125°F | 125–130°F |
| Medium-rare | 125–130°F | 130–135°F |
| Medium | 135–140°F | 140–145°F |
| Medium-well / well | 145–155°F | 150–160°F |

USDA recommends 145°F. Steak below that carries a small risk; whole muscle cuts have bacteria mostly on the surface, so searing helps; ground beef is different (160°F).

## Methods

| Method | How | Cuts |
|---|---|---|
| **Pan-sear** | pat dry, hot cast iron, 3–5 min per side, butter baste | ribeye, strip, sirloin, flat iron |
| **Reverse-sear** | 225–250°F oven to 10–15°F below target, then sear | thick steaks |
| **Grill** | high heat 3–5 min per side; two-zone | flank, skirt, sirloin, tri-tip |
| **Roast** | 425°F 15 min then 325°F | rib roast, sirloin roast, tri-tip |
| **Braise** | brown, liquid halfway up, 300–325°F 2.5–4 h | chuck, short rib, shank, brisket |
| **Slow cooker** | LOW 8–10 h | chuck, brisket, round |
| **Pressure cook** | high 60–75 min | chuck roast |
| **Stir-fry** | slice thin against the grain, high heat 2–3 min | flank, sirloin, round |
| **Smoke** | 225–250°F | brisket, ribs, chuck |
| **Stew** | simmer 1.5–3 h | chuck cubes |
| **Jerky** | 160–175°F dehydrator/oven | top/bottom round |

**Resting:** 5–10 min for steaks, 15–20 min for large roasts.
**Slicing:** always against the grain (flank, skirt, brisket).

## Seasoning pairings
Salt-and-pepper (coarse), garlic, cumin, chili powder, smoked paprika, coffee-chili rub, Worcestershire, soy, rosemary, thyme, horseradish, chimichurri, red wine reduction, mushrooms, blue cheese butter.

## Meal-prep rules
- Ground beef crumbles freeze 3–4 months; cook once, portion 1/2 lb.
- Braised beef improves on day 2 and freezes 3–4 months in its sauce.
- Do not reheat steak in a microwave; slice cold onto a bowl or briefly warm in a hot pan.
