# Turkey, Duck and Other Poultry

All poultry: **165°F** safe minimum (ground included).

## Turkey

| Form | Notes | Method |
|---|---|---|
| **Whole turkey** | 10–22 lb; thaw in the fridge (24 h per 4–5 lb) | roast 325°F; about 13 min/lb unstuffed; breast 165°F, thigh 175°F; rest 30 min |
| **Spatchcocked turkey** | cooks in ~1.5–2 h at 450°F | evenly cooked |
| **Turkey breast (bone-in)** | 4–7 lb | roast 325°F 1.5–2.5 h to 165°F |
| **Turkey breast cutlets / tenderloin** | like chicken breast | pan-sear, grill, bake |
| **Turkey thighs / drumsticks** | dark, cheap, gamey-rich | braise 2–3 h, slow cooker 6–8 h, roast 350°F 1.5–2 h |
| **Ground turkey** | blend labels: 99% (breast only), 93/7, 85/15 | skillet crumble, meatballs, chili |

**Ground turkey:** 93/7 is the useful all-round blend (about 150 kcal, 18.7 g protein, 8.3 g fat per 100 g raw, reference); 99% dries out; use only in sauces. 85/15 is juicier.
Turkey is milder than chicken, so season more assertively: sage, thyme, smoked paprika, garlic, chili powder.

## Duck
Fatty; score the skin, start skin-down in a cold pan to render, finish roasting 375°F. Breast: medium (135–140°F) is common but USDA says 165°F; leg: braise or confit. Save the rendered fat.

## Cornish hens
Small (1–1.5 lb); roast 425°F 45–55 min; one bird per person.

## Rotisserie and pre-cooked
Store rotisserie chicken often costs $5–8 for ~2 lb of meat; remove meat within 2 hours, use in 3–4 days.

## Storage
Same as chicken: raw 1–2 days fridge, 9 months frozen (pieces), cooked 3–4 days, frozen cooked ~4 months.

See [`chicken.md`](chicken.md) for cooking method details that apply to all poultry.
