# Eggs, Dairy and Plant Protein

Macros here are USDA SR Legacy reference values (per 100 g) and were not re-verified against the API this session.

## Eggs

One large egg (50 g): about 72 kcal, 6.3 g protein, 4.8 g fat.

| Style | Method | Time |
|---|---|---|
| **Hard-boiled** | lower into boiling water, boil, ice bath | 11–12 min; keep 7 days |
| **Soft-boiled** | same | 6–7 min |
| **Scrambled** | low heat, stir constantly, pull while wet | 3–4 min |
| **Fried / sunny-side** | medium heat, lid for set whites | 2–3 min |
| **Poached** | 180°F water with splash of vinegar | 3–4 min |
| **Omelet / frittata** | frittata: 375°F oven 15–20 min to 160°F | – |
| **Egg muffins** | 12-cup tin, 350°F 18–22 min | batch-prep; keeps 4 days, freezes 2–3 months |
| **Baked (shakshuka)** | simmer eggs in tomato sauce | 8–10 min |
| **Egg fried rice** | see recipes | – |

Egg dishes must reach 160°F; runny yolks carry a small risk (USDA).
Raw shell eggs: 3–5 weeks refrigerated.

## Dairy

| Food | kcal / P / F per 100 g (ref.) | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Greek yogurt, plain non-fat | 59 / 10.2 / 0.4 | overnight oats, marinades (tenderizes), sauces |
| Cottage cheese, low-fat | 72 / 12.4 / 1.0 | eggs, bowls, toast |
| Whole milk | 61 / 3.2 / 3.3 | oats, shakes |
| Skyr | ~63 / 11 / 0.2 | like Greek yogurt |
| Cheese (cheddar) | 403 / 25 / 33 | flavor, high fat |
| Parmesan | 431 / 38 / 29 | flavor; small amounts |
| Whey protein | ~80% protein | convenience |

Budget: Greek yogurt (32 oz tubs), cottage cheese, milk and eggs are among the cheapest complete proteins per gram.

## Plant protein

| Food | Dry kcal / P / C per 100 g (ref.) | Cook |
|---|---|---|
| **Lentils (brown/green)** | 352 / 24.6 / 63 | simmer 20–30 min; no soak; 1:3 ratio |
| **Red lentils** | similar | simmer 10–15 min; break down into dal/soup |
| **Black beans** | 341 / 21.6 / 62 | soak 6–8 h then simmer 60–90 min; or pressure cook 25–30 min unsoaked |
| **Chickpeas** | 364 / 19 / 61 | soak; simmer 60–90 min; pressure cook 40–50 min |
| **Kidney beans** | ~333 / 24 / 60 | must be boiled hard 10 min (lectin); then simmer 60–90 min |
| **Split peas** | 341 / 24 / 61 | simmer 45–60 min |
| **Canned beans** | – | rinse; about 1.5 cups drained per 15 oz can |
| **Tofu (firm)** | ~144 / 17 / 3 (12 g/100 g typical) | press 20 min, bake 400°F 25–30 min, pan-fry, scramble |
| **Tempeh** | ~190 / 20 / 9 | steam 10 min, then marinate and bake or pan-fry |
| **Seitan** | ~120 / 25 / 4 | simmer or bake; pure gluten |
| **Edamame** | 121 / 12 / 9 | boil 5 min |
| **Peanut butter** | 588 / 25 / 50 | high calorie, cheap |

**Protein quality:** animal proteins are complete and highly digestible. Legumes are low in methionine; rice or grains complement them. Cost about $0.02 per gram of protein for dry lentils (among the cheapest sources).
Freezing: cooked beans and lentils freeze 6 months. Season with cumin, smoked paprika, bay leaf, garlic, lemon or vinegar at the end.

## Nuts and seeds
Peanut butter, almond butter, seeds: high in fat and calories; measure with a scale when tracking.
