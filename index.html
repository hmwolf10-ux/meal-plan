Breakfast: Overnight Oats + PB + Whey

38g protein | 60g carbs | 18g fat | 458 cal

Make 7 jars on batch day. Grab one each morning.

Storage: 4-5 days refrigerated

Lunch & Dinner: Chicken Rice Bowl (2x daily)

50g protein | 65g carbs | 16g fat | 513 cal each
Chicken 10 oz
Rice 1 cup
Vegetables ½ cup (frozen or roasted)
Sauce 2.5 tsp soy + 2 tsp honey

Assembly: Scoop rice → add chicken → add veg → drizzle sauce → microwave 90s

Storage: 4 days fridge | 3 months frozen

Snack: PB Banana Shake

32g protein | 55g carbs | 16g fat | 420 cal

1 min blender time. Frozen banana is best.
