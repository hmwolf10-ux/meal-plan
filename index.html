🔄 The System

Weekly Routine

  1. Batch Cook Day (~50 minutes)
    • Bake 10 lb chicken thighs (2 sheet pans, 35-40 min)
    • Cook 5 cups rice (18 min)
    • Make 7 overnight oat jars (5 min)
  2. Weekdays (~5 minutes per meal)
    • Assemble bowls: chicken + rice + veg + sauce
    • Grab overnight oats (no prep)
    • Blend banana PB shake (90 sec)

💡 Key Principles

⚡

Protein First

Every meal hits 28-50g protein. Choose proteins on sale. Build your week around what's cheapest.

🛒

Smart Shopping

Core pantry staples + weekly protein on sale. Frozen vegetables when fresh aren't on budget.

⏱️

Batch Prep

Cook once, eat all week. Proteins, grains, and veg are your building blocks for quick assembly.

💰

Budget Tier

Every recipe is tested for cost-per-serving. Cheapest options: rice, lentils, eggs, frozen veg.
